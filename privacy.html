Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Overview

Streak Daily is designed to work privately on your device. We do not require an account, operate a remote backend for the app, or collect personal data through the app.

Data You Enter

Streak Daily lets you enter project titles, daily activity descriptions, reminder times, project statuses, and daily progress information. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. It is not transmitted to us or to a server operated by us.

Data Collection and Tracking

Streak Daily does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics, location, contacts, identifiers, or advertising data. The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics services, or third-party software development kits that collect user data.

Local Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Streak Daily schedules local reminders on your device. Reminder content and scheduling information remain on the device and are handled by iOS. You can change or revoke notification permission at any time in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Streak Daily.

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, disclose, or share user data with third parties because the app does not collect or receive user data. Apple may process information independently when you use iOS or the App Store, subject to Apple's own privacy policy and settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete individual projects or uninstall the app. Deleting a project removes its locally stored reminder and progress information. Uninstalling Streak Daily removes the app and its local data according to iOS behavior.

Because we do not receive or store your app data on a server, we cannot access, export, restore, or remotely delete it. No account-deletion request is necessary.

Security

Your information benefits from the security protections provided by iOS and your device. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security, such as a passcode and current system software.

Children's Privacy

Streak Daily does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user. The app's local-only data practices apply equally to all users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL, and the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the app or its App Store information when appropriate.
