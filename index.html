Getting started

Create a project

Tap the plus button, add a title and optional daily activity, then choose up to three reminder times.

Start a project

Open the project and tap Start. Streak Daily recommends keeping no more than three projects active at once.

Pause or finish

Pause an active project when you need a break. Completed and abandoned projects are final and stop sending reminders.

Edit reminders

Open a project, choose Edit, and update its daily times. Changes are applied to future reminders automatically.

Notification help

Not receiving reminders?

  1. Make sure the project status is In Progress.
  2. Confirm that Daily Reminders is enabled and at least one time is set.
  3. Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Streak Daily.
  4. Turn on Allow Notifications, Sounds, and the alert styles you prefer.
  5. Return to Streak Daily and reopen the project.

Why did reminders stop?

Reminders are automatically removed when a project is paused, completed, abandoned, or deleted.

Do reminders need internet?

No. Streak Daily uses local iPhone notifications and does not require a network connection.

Data and privacy

✓

Private by design

Your projects and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. Streak Daily does not require an account, include analytics, or upload your project data to a server.
